About Best Snorkeling Belize

Updated November 10, 2026

Belize has some of the most accessible reef snorkeling in the Caribbean. This guide covers the best spots, marine life, seasons and how to choose a base and an operator, with reef-safe habits throughout.

Who publishes it

Published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We don't run boats. Some local tour companies and shops pay for featured spots on the page; those are labeled.

Our standards

  • Marine reserve rules come from official sources and can change; check before you go.
  • No made-up reviews or ratings.
  • Safety first: currents, sun and boat traffic are real risks.
